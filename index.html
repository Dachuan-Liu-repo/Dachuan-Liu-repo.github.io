Dachuan Liu

Computer Engineering, University of Michigan
Mechanical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (dual degree)

I work on multi-agent path finding: computing collision-free paths for large teams of robots that share the same space. With Prof. Zhongqiang Ren at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, I develop anytime planners for agents whose actions take different amounts of time. At Michigan, I work with Prof. Steven Ceron on self-reconfigurable modular robots that move by rolling around one another.

I am applying to PhD programs in robotics for Fall 2027.

Research

2026 – now

Anytime planning for multi-agent path finding with asynchronous actions

Robotics, Autonomy, and Planning Lab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Advisor: Prof. Zhongqiang Ren

I developed LSRP*, a C++ solver that returns a feasible plan quickly and keeps improving it within a time budget, using dominance pruning and restart strategies. I am now extending it to large agents that occupy several cells at once.

2026 – now

Self-reconfigurable modular robots

Robotics Department, University of Michigan. Advisor: Prof. Steven Ceron

Each module is a steel cylinder with an internal magnet that extends, retracts, and travels around its perimeter, letting modules attach, detach, and roll around each other. I am finalizing the design and building a fleet of 20 to 25 modules, including a redesigned power system and fatigue testing of the magnet mechanism.

2026 – now

Visual demand of driving around obstacles

Multidisciplinary Design Program, University of Michigan. Advisor: Prof. Paul Green

I design Unreal Engine 5 driving experiments that vary obstacle type and curve radius, and measure drivers' visual demand with the visual occlusion method.
